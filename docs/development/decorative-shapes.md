# Decorative shapes

Flat shapes in the theme's primary colour behind the content of player-facing pages — the
landing page's wave was the first. The wave and the `Blob`s below were both removed on 2026-09-28
([`../home/landing-page.md`](../home/landing-page.md), "How it got here" §4); the landing page's
**line globe** (2026-09-29) is the one in use now. This page keeps what they taught.
They live in `src/components/shapes/`, one component per shape. Each one is decoration only:
`aria-hidden`, `pointer-events-none`, never carrying information, and **text never sits on
it** — muted or foreground text on primary is unreadable in both themes.

## Where they are

| Page | Shape | How |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | The line globe, risen over the bottom edge behind the secondary buttons | `pages/Home/hero/line-globe.tsx`, placed by `Home.tsx` — see [`landing-page.md`](../home/landing-page.md#the-globe) |

`/choose-quiz` sits on its plain page background, and `src/components/shapes/` is gone — the last
shared shape, `Blob`, was retired on 2026-09-28 (below). The landing wave's files are in
`_to_delete/wave/`.

The globe keeps the rules below: it is out of flow and under the content (`z-0` against the
pitch's `z-10`), its land is a 45% tint rather than solid primary, and the only things that sit
over its top edge are the secondary buttons — opaque surfaces — and the small "or" divider. (The
404 page's desk globe is a picture, not a background shape: it *is* the "0" — see
[`error-handling.md`](./error-handling.md), "The error screens".)

## `Blob` (retired 2026-09-28)

`Blob` was one filled, roughly round shape in `fill-primary`, in four generated outlines
(`BLOB_A`–`BLOB_D`, from `scripts/generate-blobs.py`). `/choose-quiz` scattered 18 of them in a
background layer behind the filter sidebar and card grid, blurred and faded as one layer
(`blur-2xl opacity-60`, from 2026-09-24). Removed at the owner's request, blur and all. The
component, its shapes and the generator are in `_to_delete/blob/` if it's ever wanted back.

What it taught still applies to any shape: keep it out of where the (primary) loader and empty
states render, and **make every surface that holds text over it opaque**. The filter sidebar and
the back link were translucent (`bg-card/50` with a blur, `bg-card/60`) and showed a blob through
as a blue smear under the labels; both became `bg-background` (2026-09-23) and stay that way.

### How it got here (2026-09-23)

1. **A circular donut** around the cards — replaced, too uniform.
2. **An uneven, slowly morphing ring** (a blob with a hole) — replaced, still read as an
   outline. Its files are in `_to_delete/blob-ring/`.
3. **A filled cloud** behind the card grid (`BlobCloud`, generated by
   `generate-blob-cloud.py`), on `/choose-mode` and `/multiplayer-menu` — retired. Its files are in
   `_to_delete/blob-cloud/` (and an earlier `cloud` variant in `_to_delete/cloud/`).
4. **Scattered `Blob`s** on `/choose-quiz`, blurred from 2026-09-24 — removed 2026-09-28.
   Files in `_to_delete/blob/`.

(This section and the table above described the cloud as current and on the two mode pages after
it had been retired; corrected against the code on 2026-09-23.)

## Adding one to a page

1. Behind content, never over it: shape first, content `relative`.
2. Clip overflow on the page root (`overflow-hidden`), not with `100vw` maths. A page that
   scrolls vertically still grows with its content — only the overflow is clipped.
3. Keep text off it. Check a phone (375px) and both themes.
