# Decorative shapes

Flat shapes in the theme's primary colour behind the content of player-facing pages — the
landing page's wave was the first ([`../home/landing-page.md`](../home/landing-page.md#the-wave)).
They live in `src/components/shapes/`, one component per shape. Each one is decoration only:
`aria-hidden`, `pointer-events-none`, never carrying information, and **text never sits on
it** — muted or foreground text on primary is unreadable in both themes.

## Where they are

| Page | Shape | How |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | The wave | `pages/Home/hero/wave.tsx` — page-specific, not in `shapes/` (it is masked against the copy) |
| `/choose-quiz` (`Quiz-Selection.tsx`) | `Blob` × 18, blurred | `PAGE_BLOBS` — scattered in the page's background layer, behind the filter sidebar and the card grid |

## `Blob`

`components/shapes/blob.tsx`: one filled, roughly round shape in `fill-primary`, positioned and
sized by its `className` (`absolute` is built in; give it `top-`/`left-`/… and a `w-`, and it
scales uniformly so it stays round). Four outlines, `BLOB_A`–`BLOB_D`, are generated by
`scripts/generate-blobs.py` into `blob-shapes.ts` — change the seeds or wobble there and paste the
output; don't hand-edit the paths. They fade in once and are otherwise static.

On `/choose-quiz` they sit in an `absolute inset-0 overflow-hidden` layer that is the page root's
first child, with the content in a later `relative` wrapper. Positions are percentages of the
page (which grows with the results), sizes percentages of its width, stepping down as the screen
widens. They are kept out of the upper middle of the results area, where the loader (itself
primary — it would vanish) and "No quizzes found" render, and off the toolbar row.

**Blurred, since 2026-09-24**: the whole layer carries `blur-2xl opacity-60` — one filter on the
layer rather than one per blob, so the browser composites it once. Softened, the shapes read as
light behind the cards rather than as objects, so there are more of them (18, up from 10). The
placement rule above still holds: a blurred primary patch behind the primary loader would still
wash it out.

**Every surface that holds text over this page is opaque.** The filter sidebar and the back link
used to be translucent (`bg-card/50` with a blur, `bg-card/60`): a blob behind them showed through
as a blue smear under the filter labels. Both are `bg-background` now (2026-09-23). A translucent
panel on a page with shapes is the same mistake as text on a shape.

### How it got here (2026-09-23)

1. **A circular donut** around the cards — replaced, too uniform.
2. **An uneven, slowly morphing ring** (a blob with a hole) — replaced, still read as an
   outline. Its files are in `_to_delete/blob-ring/`.
3. **A filled cloud** behind the card grid (`BlobCloud`, generated by
   `generate-blob-cloud.py`), on `/choose-mode` and `/multiplayer-menu` — retired. Its files are in
   `_to_delete/blob-cloud/` (and an earlier `cloud` variant in `_to_delete/cloud/`).
4. **Scattered `Blob`s** on `/choose-quiz` — current.

(This section and the table above described the cloud as current and on the two mode pages after
it had been retired; corrected against the code on 2026-09-23.)

## Adding one to a page

1. Behind content, never over it: shape first, content `relative`.
2. Clip overflow on the page root (`overflow-hidden`), not with `100vw` maths. A page that
   scrolls vertically still grows with its content — only the overflow is clipped.
3. Keep text off it. Check a phone (375px) and both themes.
